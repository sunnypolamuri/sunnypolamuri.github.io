Hello!

I’m Sunny Polamuri, a Data Analyst with 3 years of experience turning complex data into actionable insights that drive strategic decisions and business growth. I specialize in SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI, and Excel, with a strong focus on automating reporting workflows, improving data accuracy, and delivering impactful dashboards. I also leverage generative AI tools, like ChatGPT, to accelerate data exploration, automate queries, enhance storytelling, and streamline project documentation.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked across diverse industries. At Life.Church, I developed Tableau dashboards, implemented sentiment analysis on live chat, and created geospatial and volunteer KPI dashboards that improved adoption, engagement, and operational efficiency. During my tenure at Dividend Music Group & Dividend Sports, I built NBA player evaluation dashboards, performed real-time data scraping, and implemented regression-based player valuation models that informed scouting and strategy. At E2 Solutions, I modernized legacy reporting processes with BigQuery and Power BI, introduced role-based dashboards, and enhanced governance, accessibility, and analytics adoption across global teams.

Beyond analytics, I am a trained musician and dedicated teacher, having completed examinations with Trinity College London and taught piano and electronic keyboard to students. This experience has strengthened my ability to communicate complex concepts clearly and creatively.

I am passionate about solving problems with data, building tools that drive insights, and continuously learning new skills. Feel free to explore my projects on GitHub or connect with me on LinkedIn — I’m always open to networking, collaboration, and new challenges.
